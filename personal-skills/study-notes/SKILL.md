# Study Notes

1. Preserve the source's meaning and distinguish facts from interpretation.
2. Start with a short summary, then organize concepts, definitions, examples, and questions.
3. Link related notes using stable descriptive titles.
4. Record the source and date when later verification may matter.
5. Use callouts or highlighted blocks only for definitions, warnings, formulas, and exam-critical facts.
6. Keep unresolved questions visible rather than filling gaps with guesses.
7. When updating an existing knowledge base, merge with the relevant note instead of creating duplicates.

For meeting notes, separate decisions, action items, owners, and deadlines from
general discussion.
