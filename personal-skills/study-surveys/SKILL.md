# Study Surveys

1. Define the decision or research question the responses should support.
2. Ask only questions that contribute to that goal.
3. Use mutually exclusive answer options where one choice is expected.
4. Avoid leading, double-barreled, ambiguous, or unnecessarily personal questions.
5. Put sensitive and demographic questions last and make them optional unless required.
6. Include a short introduction explaining purpose, time, and data use.
7. Plan how each response will be summarized before publishing the form.
8. Test the form with a small sample and verify branching and required fields.

Do not collect personal or sensitive information without a clear need and appropriate consent.
